### 2.5D integration

**In plain words:** Placing several chips side by side on a shared high-density base (interposer) inside one package.

**Technically:** Side-by-side die integration on an interposer or bridge providing fine-pitch lateral interconnect.

**Related:** 3D integration, Interposer

**Sources:** [SRC-0195](https://www.imec-int.com/en/articles/chiplets-piecing-together-next-generation-chips-part-i) [SRC-0168](https://3dfabric.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/cowos.htm)

### 300mm-equivalent wafer

**In plain words:** A way of counting factory output as if every wafer were the standard large 300mm size.

**Technically:** Normalisation of wafer counts of different diameters to 300mm (12-inch) area equivalents for capacity/shipment reporting.

**Sources:** [SRC-0095](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1709048/000170904826000012/globalfoundries4q2025earni.htm) [SRC-0077](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/sec-filings/2025_20F%20Report.pdf)

### 3D integration

**In plain words:** Stacking chips on top of each other and wiring them vertically.

**Technically:** Vertical stacking of dies/wafers interconnected by TSVs, microbumps or hybrid bonds.

**Related:** Hybrid bonding, TSV

**Sources:** [SRC-0196](https://www.imec-int.com/en/expertise/cmos-advanced/connect/3d-integration) [SRC-0195](https://www.imec-int.com/en/articles/chiplets-piecing-together-next-generation-chips-part-i)

### 3D NAND

**In plain words:** Flash storage memory built as a tall stack of hundreds of layers of memory cells, like a skyscraper instead of a single floor.

**Technically:** NAND flash with memory cells stacked vertically in many word-line layers (300+ layers in production by 2024-25).

**Related:** DRAM

**Sources:** [SRC-0177](https://news.skhynix.com/sk-hynix-starts-mass-production-of-world-first-321-high-nand/)

### ABF

**In plain words:** A thin insulating film used to build the many wiring layers of the substrate that large chips sit on.

**Technically:** Epoxy-based build-up dielectric film (Ajinomoto) used in high-layer-count flip-chip substrates for CPUs, GPUs and AI accelerators.

**Related:** Package substrate, T-glass

### Advanced manufacturing investment credit

**In plain words:** A US tax credit that returns a share of the money a company spends building a chip or chip-equipment factory.

**Technically:** 26 U.S.C. 48D credit equal to 35% (25% before 2026 placements) of qualified investment in facilities primarily for manufacturing semiconductors or semiconductor manufacturing equipment; construction must begin before 2027; can be taken as direct pay.

**Sources:** [SRC-0104](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A26+section%3A48D+edition%3Aprelim%29)

### Advanced packaging

**In plain words:** Newer ways of putting chips together in one package (e.g. stacking) so they work like one bigger chip.

**Technically:** Named in 15 USC 4651 covered-facility scope; 3D integration packaging excludes a product from 'legacy' status (15 CFR 231.107).

**Sources:** [SRC-0018](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A15+section%3A4651+edition%3Aprelim%29) [SRC-0019](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-II/subchapter-C/part-231/subpart-A)

### Advanced-node IC

**In plain words:** The kinds of leading chips the US does not want made in China: the most advanced logic, DRAM and flash memory.

**Technically:** EAR definition used for end-use controls: logic at 16/14 nm or below or non-planar transistors; NAND with 128+ layers; DRAM with memory cell area < 0.0019 um2 or memory density > 0.288 Gb/mm2 (since Dec 2024, replacing 18 nm half-pitch).

**Sources:** [SRC-0279](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2022/10/13/2022-21658.txt) [SRC-0285](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2024/12/05/2024-28270.txt)

### Affiliates rule

**In plain words:** A US rule that would automatically apply blacklist restrictions to companies majority-owned by blacklisted companies.

**Technically:** BIS rule (Sept 2025) extending Entity List end-user controls to entities at least 50% owned, directly or indirectly, by listed entities; suspended 10 Nov 2025 to 9 Nov 2026.

**Sources:** [SRC-0112](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/12/2025-19846/one-year-suspension-of-expansion-of-end-user-controls-for-affiliates-of-certain-listed-entities)

### AI accelerator

**In plain words:** A chip built to do the huge number of simple maths operations that training and running AI models needs, much faster than an ordinary processor.

**Technically:** A processor optimised for dense matrix/tensor arithmetic (GPU, TPU or other ASIC) used for AI training and inference; TSMC counts AI GPUs, AI ASICs and HBM controllers for data-centre AI as 'AI accelerators'.

**Related:** GPU, XPU, High-bandwidth memory

### Analog IC

**In plain words:** Chips that handle real-world continuous signals such as power, sound or radio.

**Technically:** WSTS IC category with general-purpose and application-specific analog products.

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### Approved IC designer

**In plain words:** A chip-design company the US has vetted, so contract chipmakers can make its advanced chips without assuming they are restricted AI chips.

**Technically:** Under 90 FR 5298 (Jan 2025), designers approved by the End-User Review Committee (Supp. 6 to Part 744); 'authorized IC designers' are designers headquartered in Taiwan or Country Groups A:1/A:5 meeting criteria, subject to KYC and reporting; status extended to 31 Dec 2026.

**Related:** Approved OSAT

**Sources:** [SRC-0289](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2025/01/16/2025-00711.txt) [SRC-0290](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2026-06851.json)

### Approved OSAT

**In plain words:** A chip packaging company on a US list whose word can be relied on about what a packaged chip contains.

**Technically:** OSAT listed in Supp. 7 to Part 744 under 90 FR 5298 whose attestations (e.g. transistor count, no HBM) can overcome the 3A090.a presumption for 16/14 nm-or-below logic ICs.

**Related:** Approved IC designer, OSAT

**Sources:** [SRC-0289](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2025/01/16/2025-00711.txt)

### ASIC

**In plain words:** A chip custom-designed for one specific customer or job, rather than a general-purpose chip.

**Technically:** Application-specific integrated circuit; often developed with design-service firms offering turnkey spec-to-production flows.

**Sources:** [SRC-0219](https://www.guc-asic.com/en/about/us)

### ATM

**In plain words:** Assembly, testing and materials: ASE's core chip packaging and testing business.

**Technically:** ASE Technology Holding's segment covering semiconductor assembly (packaging), testing and packaging materials, distinct from its EMS segment.

**Sources:** [SRC-0093](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ase-technology-holding-co-ltd-reports-its-unaudited-consolidated-financial-results-for-the-fourth-quarter-and-the-full-year-of-2025-302679779.html)

### Authorized distributor

**In plain words:** A reseller officially appointed by a chipmaker to sell its chips.

**Technically:** Distributor franchised and qualified by an original component manufacturer (OCM); the OCM-sanctioned channel.

**Example:** Avnet

**Sources:** [SRC-0221](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/SIA-Anti-Counterfeiting-Whitepaper.pdf) [SRC-0215](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/8858/000110465926096346/avt-20260627x10k.htm)

### Automatic test equipment

**In plain words:** The large computer-controlled machines that electrically test chips.

**Technically:** ATE: tester systems applying test patterns and measuring responses at wafer or package level.

**Related:** Wafer sort, STDF

**Sources:** [SRC-0186](https://investors.teradyne.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000950170-25-023784/ter-20241231.htm)

### Back-end (assembly, test and packaging)

**In plain words:** The part of manufacturing that turns finished wafers into individual, tested, packaged chips that can be soldered into products.

**Technically:** Wafer probe (sometimes), dicing, die attach, interconnect, encapsulation and final test; also called ATP or ATMP.

**Related:** OSAT, Advanced packaging

**Sources:** [SRC-0153](https://web-assets.bcg.com/9d/64/367c63094411b6e9e1407bec0dcc/bcgxsia-strengthening-the-global-semiconductor-value-chain-april-2021.pdf) [SRC-0191](https://cset.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/The-Semiconductor-Supply-Chain-Issue-Brief-1.pdf)

### Backlog

**In plain words:** Orders customers have placed that have not yet been delivered or recognised as revenue.

**Technically:** Contracted but unrecognised revenue/orders; for EDA vendors akin to remaining performance obligations.

**Sources:** [SRC-0086](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q4-2025-financial-results) [SRC-0083](https://s206.q4cdn.com/597110084/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Q4-2025-CFO-Commentary-FINAL.pdf)

### Backside power delivery

**In plain words:** Moving the wires that carry power to the back of the chip, leaving more room for signal wires on the front.

**Technically:** Power delivery network fabricated on the wafer backside after thinning, connected to devices by nano-TSVs or direct contacts; e.g. Intel PowerVia, TSMC Super Power Rail (A16).

**Related:** Gate-all-around transistor

**Sources:** [SRC-0162](https://download.intel.com/newsroom/2021/client-computing/accelerating-process-innovation.pdf) [SRC-0167](https://www.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/logic/l_A16) [SRC-0196](https://www.imec-int.com/en/expertise/cmos-advanced/connect/3d-integration)

### Big Fund

**In plain words:** China's state-backed investment fund for its chip industry.

**Technically:** China Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund: phase 1 (2014, CNY 138.7bn), phase 2 (2019, CNY 204bn), phase 3 (2024, CNY 344bn registered capital).

**Sources:** [SRC-0068](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202405/29/content_WS66569746c6d0868f4e8e7987.html) [SRC-0067](https://www.usitc.gov/publications/332/journals/chinese_semiconductor_industrial_policy_past_and_present_jice_july_2019.pdf)

### Blue Book (WSTS)

**In plain words:** WSTS's detailed monthly statistics report for members and subscribers, the source of the official chip-market history.

**Technically:** WSTS monthly statistical report by product and region; the free Historical Billings Report republishes its regional totals from 1986. The Green Book contains graphs from the Blue Book.

**Related:** WSTS, Historical Billings Report

**Sources:** [SRC-0224](https://wsts.org/67/Historical-Billings-Report) [SRC-0225](https://semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Green-Book-v18_Sample.pdf)

### Bureau of Industry and Security

**In plain words:** The US Commerce Department office that decides which technology exports need a licence.

**Technically:** Agency of the US Department of Commerce administering the EAR, the Entity List and export licensing.

**Sources:** [SRC-0110](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2022/10/13/2022-21658/implementation-of-additional-export-controls-certain-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-manufacturing-items) [SRC-0114](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-revises-license-review-policy-semiconductors-exported-china)

### Burn-in

**In plain words:** Running chips hot and under stress for a while so weak ones fail in the factory instead of in customers' hands.

**Technically:** Accelerated-stress screening at elevated temperature/voltage to remove early-life failures.

**Related:** Final test

**Sources:** [SRC-0187](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/0_2015-ITRS-2.0-Test-.pdf) [SRC-0189](https://www.pdf.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-data-101-an-introduction/)

### Capacity reservation

**In plain words:** A promise by a factory to hold production space for a particular customer.

**Technically:** Commitment by a foundry to reserve wafer capacity for a customer, typically in exchange for customer volume commitments and/or prepayments.

**Sources:** [SRC-0094](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1709048/000170904826000022/gfs-20251231.htm) [SRC-0077](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/sec-filings/2025_20F%20Report.pdf)

### Capital expenditures

**In plain words:** Money spent on long-lasting assets such as factories and equipment.

**Technically:** Purchases of property, plant and equipment (and sometimes intangibles); may be reported gross or net of government incentives and partner contributions.

**Sources:** [SRC-0077](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/sec-filings/2025_20F%20Report.pdf) [SRC-0099](https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0000050863-26-000009/q425earningsrelease.htm)

### Capital intensity

**In plain words:** How much a company must spend on factories and machines compared with what it sells.

**Technically:** Capital expenditures as a share of revenue.

**Sources:** [SRC-0010](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-SIA-Factbook-FINAL-1.pdf)

### Captive mask shop

**In plain words:** A photomask factory owned by a chipmaker to make its own masks, as opposed to an independent mask supplier.

**Technically:** In-house photomask manufacturing operated by an IDM or foundry, versus merchant mask makers.

**Related:** Photomask

**Sources:** [SRC-0183](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/810136/000114036125045801/ef20057458_10k.htm)

### Chiplet

**In plain words:** A small chip designed to be combined with other small chips in one package, instead of making one huge chip.

**Technically:** Die implementing a subset of system functions, designed for integration with other dies via advanced packaging and die-to-die interfaces (e.g. UCIe).

**Related:** Advanced packaging, UCIe, Known good die

**Sources:** [SRC-0185](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2023/11/19/NAPMP-Vision-Paper-20231120.pdf) [SRC-0163](https://newsroom.intel.com/tech101/explaining-common-chip-terms) [SRC-0195](https://www.imec-int.com/en/articles/chiplets-piecing-together-next-generation-chips-part-i)

### CHIPS Act

**In plain words:** A 2022 US law that pays grants and offers loans and tax credits to companies building chip factories in the US.

**Technically:** CHIPS and Science Act: US$39bn manufacturing incentives (direct funding, loans) administered by Commerce/NIST, plus the 48D investment tax credit.

**Sources:** [SRC-0105](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-07-14_R49031_157331cbaf561ee49780ef5b60b2f38407b33cde.html)

### Chokepoint

**In plain words:** A step in the supply chain where one region or a few firms supply almost everything, so a disruption there hits everyone.

**Technically:** SIA/BCG definition: a value-chain step where one region accounts for more than 65% of global market share.

**Sources:** [SRC-0031](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/SIA-BCG_Global-Value-Chain_2-pager.pdf) [SRC-0199](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/BCG-x-SIA-Strengthening-the-Global-Semiconductor-Value-Chain-April-2021_1.pdf)

### Conflict minerals

**In plain words:** Tin, tantalum, tungsten and gold that may come from conflict areas in central Africa; US-listed companies must report on them.

**Technically:** Minerals covered by Dodd-Frank Section 1502 and SEC Form SD reporting for sourcing from the DRC and adjoining countries.

**Sources:** [SRC-0150](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/conflict-minerals-disclosure)

### Consensus forecast (WSTS)

**In plain words:** WSTS's twice-yearly market forecast, built by pooling member companies' own outlooks and agreeing them at a meeting.

**Technically:** Semi-annual (spring, autumn) forecast covering the current and following years quarterly and two further years annually, from member-submitted outlooks, statistical analysis and a members' forecast meeting.

**Related:** WSTS

**Sources:** [SRC-0228](https://wsts.org/65/DATA-RECIPROCITY) [SRC-0226](https://semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/WSTS-Presentation-Subscription-Oct2024.pdf)

### Consigned wafer

**In plain words:** A customer's wafer that is sent to a packaging company but still belongs to the customer.

**Technically:** Material supplied by the customer to a service provider where title and risk of loss remain with the customer.

**Sources:** [SRC-0092](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1047127/000104712726000014/amkr-20251231.htm)

### Country Group D:5

**In plain words:** A US list of countries under a US arms embargo, including China; many chip-tool rules apply to exports to these countries.

**Technically:** Country group in Supplement No. 1 to 15 CFR Part 740 (US arms-embargoed countries, incl. the PRC); used with Macau as destination scope for the SME FDP rule and ECAD/TCAD controls.

**Sources:** [SRC-0284](https://media.bis.gov/sites/default/files/documents/FINAL%20DOC%20Nat%20Sec%20Action%20Rls%20Dec%202%2024.pdf) [SRC-0305](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-01/title-15.xml?part=734&section=734.9)

### CoWoS

**In plain words:** TSMC's packaging technology that puts processors and HBM memory side by side on an interposer; used for most large AI accelerators.

**Technically:** Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate: TSMC 2.5D platform with silicon (S), RDL (R) or local-silicon-bridge (L) interposers up to ~3.5x reticle size.

**Related:** Interposer, HBM

**Sources:** [SRC-0168](https://3dfabric.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/cowos.htm)

### Critical dimension

**In plain words:** The size of the smallest feature (e.g. line width) that must be printed accurately on a layer.

**Technically:** Smallest feature width controlled on a layer; measured by CD metrology.

**Related:** Rayleigh criterion, Metrology

**Sources:** [SRC-0157](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/rayleigh-criterion) [SRC-0189](https://www.pdf.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-data-101-an-introduction/)

### Currency effect (WSTS regional data)

**In plain words:** Because WSTS counts everything in US dollars, a region's market can shrink in dollars while growing in its local currency when that currency weakens.

**Technically:** E.g. Japan 2023: -2.9% in USD (Blue Book) but +3.8% in yen (WSTS Japan Council) with the yen at 140.4 per dollar.

**Related:** Region of sale (WSTS)

**Sources:** [SRC-0246](https://www.jeita.or.jp/japanese/stat/wsts/docs/20241203WSTS.pdf) [SRC-0223](https://www.wsts.org/esraCMS/extension/media/f/WST/7771/WSTS-Historical-Billings-Report-Jul_2026.xlsx)

### Cycle time

**In plain words:** How long it takes for a wafer to go through the factory from start to finish.

**Technically:** Elapsed time for a wafer lot to complete front-end processing (roughly 12 weeks on average per BCG/SIA 2021).

**Related:** Lot

**Sources:** [SRC-0153](https://web-assets.bcg.com/9d/64/367c63094411b6e9e1407bec0dcc/bcgxsia-strengthening-the-global-semiconductor-value-chain-april-2021.pdf)

### DAO

**In plain words:** Shorthand for the 'simpler', often older-technology chips: discretes, analog, optoelectronics and sensors.

**Technically:** Discretes, analog, and optoelectronics & sensors (SIA/BCG usage).

**Sources:** [SRC-0030](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Emerging-Resilience-in-the-Semiconductor-Supply-Chain_BCG-Briefing.pdf)

### Data collection agent (DCA)

**In plain words:** An accounting firm that collects each chip company's confidential sales numbers for WSTS, checks them and adds them up anonymously.

**Technically:** Regional DCAs (Moss Adams, topserve.ch, Grant Thornton Taiyo in 2019) compile member submissions, check completeness and plausibility, anonymise and pass them to a worldwide DCA; they tabulate without audit and impute missing reports from prior market share.

**Related:** WSTS

**Sources:** [SRC-0225](https://semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Green-Book-v18_Sample.pdf) [SRC-0226](https://semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/WSTS-Presentation-Subscription-Oct2024.pdf)

### Data-center GPU

**In plain words:** Originally a chip for drawing graphics; big versions are now the main general-purpose chip used to train and run AI in data centres.

**Technically:** A massively parallel processor sold by merchant vendors (NVIDIA, AMD) as a programmable AI accelerator, usually packaged with HBM on a 2.5D interposer and sold as boards, servers or full racks.

**Related:** AI accelerator, CoWoS

### De minimis rule (EAR)

**In plain words:** A US rule saying that a product made abroad falls under US export rules if it contains more than a small share of controlled US parts; for some chipmaking tools that share is zero.

**Technically:** 15 CFR 734.4: foreign-made items incorporating controlled US content above 10% (E:1/E:2) or 25% (other destinations) of value are subject to the EAR; 734.4(a)(8) sets no de minimis level for specified SME containing US-origin ICs destined to Macau/D:5.

**Related:** Foreign Direct Product Rule

**Sources:** [SRC-0307](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-01/title-15.xml?part=734&section=734.4)

### Deposition

**In plain words:** Laying down an ultra-thin layer of material on the wafer.

**Technically:** Thin-film formation by CVD, PVD, ALD, epitaxy or electroplating.

**Related:** Etch

**Sources:** [SRC-0159](https://www.asml.com/en/news/stories/2021/semiconductor-manufacturing-process-steps)

### Design rules

**In plain words:** Geometric rules (minimum widths, spacings, etc.) a chip layout must follow so the factory can build it reliably.

**Technically:** Process-specific layout constraints checked by DRC before tape-out.

**Related:** PDK, Signoff

**Sources:** [SRC-0178](https://github.com/google/skywater-pdk)

### Die

**In plain words:** One individual chip cut out of a finished wafer.

**Technically:** A single rectangular IC cut from a processed wafer by dicing.

**Related:** Wafer, Chiplet, Yield

**Sources:** [SRC-0163](https://newsroom.intel.com/tech101/explaining-common-chip-terms)

### Direct pay

**In plain words:** An option that lets a company receive a tax credit as a cash payment instead of only reducing tax owed.

**Technically:** Elective payment under 48D(d): credit treated as a payment against tax, refundable.

**Sources:** [SRC-0104](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A26+section%3A48D+edition%3Aprelim%29)

### Discrete semiconductor

**In plain words:** A single-function electronic part such as one diode or transistor, not a whole circuit.

**Technically:** WSTS category covering diodes, small-signal and power transistors, rectifiers, thyristors and other discretes.

**Related:** DAO

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### Distribution inventory days

**In plain words:** How many days of sales worth of a chipmaker's products are sitting in its distributors' warehouses.

**Technically:** Inventory held in the distribution channel expressed in days of sell-through.

**Sources:** [SRC-0102](https://ir.microchip.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1309/microchip-technology-announces-financial-results-for-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2025)

### DRAM

**In plain words:** The fast working memory a computer uses while it is running; loses data when power is off.

**Technically:** Dynamic random-access memory; a WSTS memory subcategory.

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### Dual-use items

**In plain words:** Products or technology that can be used for both civilian and military purposes.

**Technically:** Goods, software and technology with civil and military applications, controlled e.g. under Regulation (EU) 2021/821 and the Wassenaar list.

**Sources:** [SRC-0128](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/news/2026-update-eu-control-list-dual-use-items-2026-09-14_en) [SRC-0136](https://www.wassenaar.org/about-us/)

### Dumping

**In plain words:** Selling products abroad below their cost or home-market price.

**Technically:** Export pricing below 'fair market value'; the 1986 US-Japan Arrangement set company-specific foreign market values for DRAMs and EPROMs.

**Sources:** [SRC-0047](https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-87-134br.pdf)

### DUV lithography

**In plain words:** An older but still vital chip-printing machine type using ultraviolet light; subject to Dutch export controls since 2023.

**Technically:** Lithography at 248nm/193nm wavelengths (including 193nm immersion) used for most layers and for older nodes.

**Sources:** [SRC-0072](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2024/09/06/the-netherlands-expands-export-control-measure-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment)

### ECCN

**In plain words:** A code that tells an exporter which export rules apply to a specific product.

**Technically:** Five-character alphanumeric classification on the US Commerce Control List (e.g. 3A090) identifying control parameters and reasons for control.

**Sources:** [SRC-0111](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/12/05/2024-28270/foreign-produced-direct-product-rule-additions-and-refinements-to-controls-for-advanced-computing)

### Electronic system design (EDA)

**In plain words:** Software tools engineers use to design and check chips and circuit boards.

**Technically:** ESD Alliance scope: CAE, IC physical design & verification, PCB & MCM tools, semiconductor IP and services.

**Sources:** [SRC-0022](https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/esd-alliance-reports-electronic-system-design-industry-posts-5.5-billion-dollars-in-revenue-in-q4-2025)

### EMS

**In plain words:** Electronics manufacturing services: building electronic modules or products for other companies.

**Technically:** Contract assembly of electronic systems/modules; a separate segment at ASE (USI).

**Sources:** [SRC-0093](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ase-technology-holding-co-ltd-reports-its-unaudited-consolidated-financial-results-for-the-fourth-quarter-and-the-full-year-of-2025-302679779.html)

### End use

**In plain words:** What kind of final product a chip ends up in (computer, phone, car, factory machine, etc.).

**Technically:** WSTS annual End Use Survey segmentation: PC/computer, communications, consumer, automotive, industrial, government (incl. military).

**Sources:** [SRC-0010](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-SIA-Factbook-FINAL-1.pdf) [SRC-0009](https://www.semiconductors.org/ai-auto-industrial-markets-spurred-rebound-in-chip-demand-during-second-half-of-2023/)

### Entity List

**In plain words:** A US government blacklist: selling US technology to listed companies needs a special licence.

**Technically:** List maintained by BIS under the Export Administration Regulations; exports of items subject to the EAR to listed parties require a licence, often with a presumption of denial.

### Entity List footnote designations (1, 4, 5)

**In plain words:** Marks next to some names on the US trade blacklist that extend US control to foreign-made goods going to those companies.

**Technically:** Footnotes in Supp. 4 to Part 744 triggering entity-specific FDP rules in 734.9(e): footnote 1 (Huawei, 2020), footnote 4 (advanced computing/supercomputer entities, 2022), footnote 5 (advanced-node IC production entities, Dec 2024).

**Related:** Entity List, Foreign Direct Product Rule

### Etch

**In plain words:** Removing material where the pattern says so, to carve the circuit into the layer below.

**Technically:** Selective removal of material (dry plasma or wet chemical) through a patterned resist or hard mask.

### European Chips Act

**In plain words:** The EU law that funds chip research and lets governments support new chip factories in Europe.

**Technically:** Regulation (EU) 2023/1781, in force 21 Sept 2023, with three pillars: Chips for Europe Initiative, security of supply (first-of-a-kind facilities) and monitoring/crisis response.

### EUV lithography

**In plain words:** A machine that uses very short-wavelength light to print the tiniest chip patterns; only one company (ASML) makes it.

**Technically:** Lithography using 13.5nm wavelength light, enabling leading-edge patterning; entered commercial production around 2019.

### Excimer gas

**In plain words:** A special gas mixture (including neon) used to make the laser light in older chip-printing machines.

**Technically:** Rare-gas/halogen mixture powering excimer lasers in DUV lithography; neon is the main buffer gas.

### Expansion clawback

**In plain words:** A condition that makes a company give back US chip subsidies if it significantly expands chip factories in China or other countries of concern.

**Technically:** 15 CFR Part 231 restriction on CHIPS recipients' material expansion of semiconductor capacity in foreign countries of concern for 10 years after award.

### Export Administration Regulations

**In plain words:** The main US rulebook deciding which goods, software and technology need a government licence before they can be sent abroad.

**Technically:** US regulations (15 CFR Parts 730-774) administered by BIS that control exports, re-exports and in-country transfers of dual-use items listed on the Commerce Control List and items subject to end-use/end-user controls.

### Export controls

**In plain words:** Government rules that stop certain goods or technology being sold to certain countries or companies without permission.

**Technically:** Licensing regimes restricting exports, re-exports and transfers of controlled items and services (e.g. US BIS rules of Oct 2022 on advanced computing and chipmaking equipment).

### Fab

**In plain words:** A chip factory, where circuits are built onto silicon wafers.

**Technically:** A semiconductor wafer fabrication facility with cleanrooms and process tools (lithography, deposition, etch, etc.).

### Fabless

**In plain words:** A chip company that designs and sells chips but pays others to manufacture them.

**Technically:** Company that outsources wafer fabrication, packaging and test while owning the product and selling under its own brand.

### Fault detection and classification

**In plain words:** Software that watches sensor readings from factory machines to spot problems early.

**Technically:** FDC: statistical monitoring of equipment sensor traces to detect and classify process excursions.

**Related:** MES

### Final test

**In plain words:** Testing each chip again after it has been packaged, before it ships.

**Technically:** Package-level test on ATE with handlers, possibly including burn-in and system-level test.

**Related:** Wafer sort, System-level test

### FinFET

**In plain words:** A 3D transistor design, shaped like a fin, used in most advanced chips from about 2011 to the early 2020s.

**Technically:** Multi-gate MOSFET where the gate wraps three sides of a vertical silicon fin channel; introduced in volume by Intel in 2011.

**Related:** Gate-all-around transistor

### First-of-a-kind facility

**In plain words:** A chip factory that is new to Europe in its technology or type, which qualifies for special EU support.

**Technically:** Chips Act concept for integrated production facilities or open EU foundries not yet substantially present in the Union; basis for State aid approvals.

### Foreign Direct Product Rule

**In plain words:** A US rule that lets the US control some products made outside the US if they were made using US technology or US-made machines.

**Technically:** EAR provisions that subject foreign-produced items to the EAR when they are the direct product of specified US-origin technology or software, or of plants/major components that are themselves such products.

**Sources:** [SRC-0110](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2022/10/13/2022-21658/implementation-of-additional-export-controls-certain-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-manufacturing-items) [SRC-0111](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/12/05/2024-28270/foreign-produced-direct-product-rule-additions-and-refinements-to-controls-for-advanced-computing)

### Foundry

**In plain words:** A factory company that manufactures chips designed by other companies.

**Technically:** Contract wafer manufacturer serving fabless and system companies; does not sell own-brand chips.

**In plain words:** TSMC's wider definition of its market, adding packaging, testing, mask-making and non-memory chipmakers' own factories.

### Franchised distribution agreement

**In plain words:** An official agreement letting a distributor resell a chipmaker's products, usually with protection if prices fall or products become obsolete.

**Technically:** Authorised distribution contract typically providing price-erosion and obsolescence protection, cancellable on notice, with inventory return rights on termination.

### Front-end manufacturing

**In plain words:** The part of manufacturing where the circuits are built into the wafer, layer by layer, inside the fab.

**Technically:** Wafer fabrication: the repeated deposition, lithography, etch, implant, CMP and metrology steps that build transistors and interconnect on the wafer.

### Gallium and germanium

**In plain words:** Rare metals used to make some kinds of chips; China produces most of the world's supply.

### Gallium nitride (GaN)

**In plain words:** A semiconductor material, different from silicon, that makes efficient chips for power supplies and chargers.

**Technically:** Wide-bandgap III-V compound semiconductor used for power and RF devices, often grown on silicon substrates.

### Gate-all-around transistor

**In plain words:** The newest transistor design, where the controlling gate fully surrounds stacked thin channels, giving better control and lower power.

**Technically:** GAAFET: channels formed as stacked horizontal nanosheets/nanowires surrounded on all sides by the gate; e.g. Samsung MBCFET, TSMC N2 nanosheet, Intel RibbonFET.

### GDSII

**In plain words:** The long-standing file format that stores a chip's physical layout (the shapes on each layer).

### Glass substrate

**In plain words:** A new kind of package base made from glass instead of plastic-like materials, flatter and able to carry denser wiring.

### High Performance Computing

**In plain words:** TSMC's revenue category for chips that go into powerful computers such as AI servers and PCs.

**In plain words:** The next generation of EUV machines with larger optics that can print even smaller features (about 8 nm).

### Hybrid bonding

**In plain words:** Joining two chips (or wafers) face to face so their copper contacts fuse directly, without solder bumps, giving extremely dense connections.

### Hyperscaler

**In plain words:** One of the few giant cloud companies (such as Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Meta) that run enormous data centres and buy most AI chips.

### IDM

**In plain words:** A chip company that both designs and manufactures its own chips in its own factories.

**Technically:** Integrated device manufacturer: owns design, wafer processing, packaging and test.

### Industrial policy

**In plain words:** When governments use money, rules or tax breaks to build up specific industries in their country.

### Ingot

### Installed base management

**In plain words:** Selling services, upgrades and spare parts for machines a customer already owns.

**Technically:** ASML's term for service and field-option (upgrade) sales to its installed base of systems.

### Integrated circuit

**In plain words:** A 'chip': many tiny electronic parts built together on one piece of semiconductor.

**Technically:** Monolithic, hybrid, multichip or multi-component circuit whose elements are formed in and on a semiconductor (HS 8542, Note 12(b)); WSTS subdivides ICs into analog, micro, logic and memory.

**Related:** Logic, Memory, Analog IC, Micro

**Sources:** [SRC-0017](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2022/c85.html) [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### JEDEC

### Memory

**In plain words:** Chips that store data.

**Technically:** WSTS IC category including DRAM, SRAM, flash (NAND/NOR) and ROM/EPROM.

**Related:** DRAM, NAND flash, HBM

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### Memory bandwidth density

**In plain words:** How fast data can move in and out of a memory chip for each square millimetre of its area; the US uses it to decide which high-bandwidth memory needs an export licence.

**Technically:** ECCN 3A090.c parameter: HBM with memory bandwidth density greater than 2 GB/s per mm2 is controlled (December 2024).

**Related:** High-bandwidth memory

**Sources:** [SRC-0285](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2024/12/05/2024-28270.txt)

### Memory cycle

**In plain words:** The repeating pattern where memory chip prices soar in shortages and crash in gluts.

**Technically:** Periodic supply-demand imbalance in commodity memory (DRAM/NAND) driven by lumpy capacity additions; e.g. 1985 collapse, 2008 declines, 2023 downturn, 2024 rebound.

**Sources:** [SRC-0066](https://www.nber.org/system/files/chapters/c8717/c8717.pdf) [SRC-0056](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-fell-by-2-8-percent-in-2008/) [SRC-0054](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-increase-19-1-in-2024-double-digit-growth-projected-in-2025/)

### MES

**In plain words:** The factory software that tracks every wafer, machine and step in a fab or packaging plant.

**Technically:** Manufacturing execution system: WIP/lot tracking, dispatch, recipe management, genealogy and equipment state.

**Related:** Lot, SECS/GEM

**Sources:** [SRC-0189](https://www.pdf.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-data-101-an-introduction/) [SRC-0190](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/opcenter/execution/semiconductor/)

### Metrology

**In plain words:** Precise measurement of what was built on the wafer (thickness, line width, alignment).

**Technically:** Inline measurement of film thickness, CD, overlay and 3D shape for process control.

**Related:** Critical dimension

**Sources:** [SRC-0189](https://www.pdf.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-data-101-an-introduction/)

### Micro

**In plain words:** The WSTS group for processors: microprocessors (CPUs), microcontrollers and digital signal processors.

**Technically:** WSTS 'MOS Micro' category: MPU, MCU, DSP.

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### Microprocessor

**In plain words:** The 'brain' of a computer built on a single chip.

**Technically:** An integrated circuit implementing a central processing unit; Intel's 4004 (1971) is commonly cited as the first commercial example.

**Sources:** [SRC-0035](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/microprocessor-integrates-cpu-function-onto-a-single-chip/)

### Military-civil fusion

**In plain words:** China's policy of linking civilian companies and research to its military; the US cites it as a reason to restrict Chinese chip firms.

**Technically:** PRC strategy integrating civilian and defence industrial bases; cited by BIS in the SMIC listing (2020) and the December 2024 Entity List rule.

**Sources:** [SRC-0277](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2020/12/22/2020-28031.txt) [SRC-0297](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2024-07004,2024-19633,2024-28267,2025-00636.json?fields[]=document_number&fields[]=citation&fields[]=publication_date&fields[]=effective_on&fields[]=abstract)

### Moore's Law

**In plain words:** The observation that the number of parts that fit on a chip doubles roughly every two years.

**Technically:** Gordon Moore's 1965 projection of component count doubling every 12 months, revised in 1975 to every two years; an industry roadmap target rather than a physical law.

**Sources:** [SRC-0036](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/moores-law-predicts-the-future-of-integrated-circuits/)

### NAND flash

**In plain words:** Memory that keeps data without power, used in phones and SSDs.

**Technically:** Non-volatile flash memory in NAND architecture; layer count (e.g. <128 layers) is used by US regulation to separate legacy from advanced NAND.

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf) [SRC-0019](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-II/subchapter-C/part-231/subpart-A)

### Net billings

**In plain words:** The value of chips a chip company invoices its customers in a period, after returns and credits.

**Technically:** WSTS reporting basis: monthly net billings (shipments) between semiconductor manufacturers and end customers, authorised distributors and divisions or subsidiaries that make end products, valued at actual sales price or comparable market price for internal transfers, including NRE.

**Related:** Region of sale (WSTS), Non-recurring engineering (NRE)

**Sources:** [SRC-0225](https://semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Green-Book-v18_Sample.pdf)

### Non-captive manufacturer

**In plain words:** A chip maker that sells chips to other companies; 'captive' chips are made only for the maker's own products and are left out of WSTS figures.

**Technically:** WSTS statistics 'reflect worldwide statistics for all non-captive semiconductor manufacturers worldwide'; Gartner by contrast ranks system companies such as Apple on in-house chips.

**Related:** WSTS

**Sources:** [SRC-0225](https://semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Green-Book-v18_Sample.pdf) [SRC-0235](https://gcom.pdo.aws.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-01-12-gartner-says-worldwide-semiconductor-revenue-grew-21-percent-in-2025)

### Non-recurring engineering (NRE)

**In plain words:** One-time fees a customer pays for designing a custom chip.

**Technically:** NRE charges for custom/semi-custom devices are included in WSTS reported value.

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### Numerical aperture

**In plain words:** A measure of how much light the lens or mirror system can gather; bigger means finer detail can be printed.

**Technically:** NA = n·sin(θ) of the projection optics; appears in the Rayleigh resolution criterion CD = k1·λ/NA.

**Related:** Rayleigh criterion, High-NA EUV

**Sources:** [SRC-0157](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/rayleigh-criterion)

### OASIS

**In plain words:** A newer, much more compact file format for chip layout and mask data, standardized by SEMI.

**Technically:** Open Artwork System Interchange Standard (SEMI P39): 64-bit hierarchical mask layout format with at least order-of-magnitude size reduction versus GDSII.

**Related:** GDSII

**Sources:** [SRC-0180](https://store-us.semi.org/products/p03900-semi-p39-specification-for-oasis%C2%AE-open-artwork-system-interchange-standard)

### Open market (broker channel)

**In plain words:** Traders who buy and resell chips outside the chipmaker's official channel; useful in shortages but a counterfeit risk.

**Technically:** Independent distributors, brokers and online component exchanges sourcing from diverse, non-OCM suppliers.

**Sources:** [SRC-0221](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/SIA-Anti-Counterfeiting-Whitepaper.pdf)

### Optoelectronics

**In plain words:** Chips that make or sense light, such as LEDs, laser diodes and image sensors.

**Technically:** WSTS category: displays (character displays), lamps, couplers/isolators, image sensors, infrared, laser and light sensors; excludes liquid crystal devices and displays.

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### OSAT

**In plain words:** A company that packages and tests chips for other companies.

**Technically:** Outsourced semiconductor assembly and test: back-end services (packaging, test) for third parties.

**Sources:** [SRC-0028](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/news-events/tech-blog/from-foundry-to-fabless-an-overview-of-the-semiconductor-ecosystem/)

### Outbound investment rules

**In plain words:** US rules limiting how Americans can invest in Chinese companies in sensitive technologies such as chips.

**Technically:** Treasury program (31 CFR Part 850, effective 2 Jan 2025) prohibiting or requiring notification of US persons' investments in covered foreign persons in semiconductors, quantum and AI in China, Hong Kong and Macau.

**Sources:** [SRC-0123](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/international/outbound-investment-program)

### Package substrate

**In plain words:** The small multilayer board a chip (or chips) sits on inside its package, connecting it to the circuit board.

**Technically:** Organic (or emerging glass-core) laminate with fine wiring that fans out die I/O to the PCB.

**Related:** Glass substrate

**Sources:** [SRC-0185](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2023/11/19/NAPMP-Vision-Paper-20231120.pdf) [SRC-0164](https://download.intel.com/newsroom/archive/2025/en-us-2023-09-18-intel-unveils-industryleading-glass-substrates-to-meet-demand-for-more-powerful-compute.pdf)

### PDK

**In plain words:** The 'rulebook and parts kit' a foundry gives designers so their designs can be manufactured on its process.

**Technically:** Process design kit: foundry-supplied design rules, device models, parameterized cells, standard-cell libraries and EDA tech files/runsets for a specific process.

**Related:** Design rules, EDA

**Sources:** [SRC-0178](https://github.com/google/skywater-pdk)

### Performance density

**In plain words:** How much computing a chip does per square millimetre of silicon; it stops companies dodging limits by splitting power across smaller chips.

**Technically:** EAR parameter = TPP divided by applicable die area (mm2); 3A090.a captures ICs with TPP >= 1600 and performance density >= 5.92.

**Related:** Total processing performance (TPP)

**Sources:** [SRC-0287](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/search/v1/results?query=%22performance%20density%22%205.92&per_page=5) [SRC-0283](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/bis-updated-public-information-page-export-controls-imposed-advanced-computing-semiconductor)

### Photolithography

**In plain words:** Printing a circuit pattern onto a wafer with light, like projecting a stencil onto light-sensitive film.

**Technically:** Pattern transfer by projecting light through a photomask onto photoresist, followed by development; resolution set by CD = k1·λ/NA.

**Related:** Photomask, Photoresist, EUV, DUV

**Sources:** [SRC-0153](https://web-assets.bcg.com/9d/64/367c63094411b6e9e1407bec0dcc/bcgxsia-strengthening-the-global-semiconductor-value-chain-april-2021.pdf) [SRC-0157](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/rayleigh-criterion)

### Photomask

**In plain words:** A glass plate carrying the exact pattern for one layer of a chip, used like a stencil in lithography.

**Technically:** High-precision quartz plate with an absorber pattern (reflective multilayer for EUV) representing one layer of an IC design; in steppers/scanners called a reticle.

**Related:** Mask set, Photolithography

**Sources:** [SRC-0183](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/810136/000114036125045801/ef20057458_10k.htm) [SRC-0153](https://web-assets.bcg.com/9d/64/367c63094411b6e9e1407bec0dcc/bcgxsia-strengthening-the-global-semiconductor-value-chain-april-2021.pdf) [SRC-0157](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/rayleigh-criterion)

### Photoresist

**In plain words:** A light-sensitive coating put on the wafer so the projected pattern can be 'developed' into it.

**Technically:** Polymer (or metal-oxide) film whose solubility changes on exposure, enabling pattern transfer to underlying layers by etch or implant.

**Related:** Photolithography

**Sources:** [SRC-0159](https://www.asml.com/en/news/stories/2021/semiconductor-manufacturing-process-steps)

### Place and route

**In plain words:** Automatically deciding where every gate sits on the chip and how the wires connect them.

**Technically:** Physical implementation: floorplanning, placement, clock-tree synthesis and routing producing a layout.

**Related:** Logic synthesis, Signoff

**Sources:** [SRC-0179](https://news.synopsys.com/2018-03-19-Synopsys-Introduces-Breakthrough-Fusion-Technology-to-Transform-the-RTL-to-GDSII-Flow)

### Planar process

**In plain words:** A way of making chips as flat layers on a silicon slice, which made mass production of integrated circuits possible.

**Technically:** Fabrication method (Hoerni, Fairchild, 1959) using a protective silicon-dioxide layer and diffusion through oxide windows so all components are made from one side of the wafer.

**Sources:** [SRC-0038](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/invention-of-the-planar-manufacturing-process/)

### Preliminary vs final estimate (Gartner)

**In plain words:** Gartner publishes a first estimate of last year's chip market in January and a revised one around April; the two can differ by several percent.

**Technically:** Gartner vendor revenue: preliminary results in January, final market share results in April; e.g. 2024 revised from US$626bn to US$655.9bn largely via a ~US$30bn Nvidia upgrade.

**Related:** Market share

**Sources:** [SRC-0238](https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/gartner-upgrades-nvidia-drops-infineon)

### Presumption of denial

**In plain words:** A licensing policy where the government expects to say no to an export application unless there is a strong reason.

**Technically:** Licence review policy under which applications are presumed to be denied; contrasted with case-by-case review.

**Sources:** [SRC-0113](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/15/2026-00789/revision-to-license-review-policy-for-advanced-computing-commodities)

### Price protection

**In plain words:** A promise by a supplier to compensate a distributor if the supplier cuts the price of stock the distributor already holds.

**Technically:** Supplier credit to distributors for price reductions on inventory on hand (Avnet describes 'protections for ... price erosion').

**Sources:** [SRC-0103](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/8858/000000885825000028/avt-20250628x10k.htm)

### Process node

**In plain words:** A generation name for a chip manufacturing technology (e.g. '3nm'); smaller usually means newer and denser, but the number is a label, not a measurement.

**Technically:** Technology generation label; no longer corresponds to any specific physical dimension.

**Sources:** [SRC-0027](https://spectrum.ieee.org/amp/the-status-of-moores-law-its-complicated-2650270098) [SRC-0024](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-01/00fe50f72b38d74e6b9b066398f020f337cd4e9d/4Q25%20Management%20Report.pdf)

### R&D intensity

**In plain words:** How much a company spends on research and new product development compared with what it sells.

**Technically:** Research and development expense as a share of revenue.

**Sources:** [SRC-0010](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-SIA-Factbook-FINAL-1.pdf)

### Rayleigh criterion

**In plain words:** The rule of thumb that says how small a feature a lithography machine can print, based on the light's wavelength and the optics.

**Technically:** CD = k1·λ/NA, where CD is critical dimension, λ wavelength, NA numerical aperture and k1 a process factor with physical limit 0.25.

**Related:** Critical dimension, Numerical aperture

**Sources:** [SRC-0157](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/rayleigh-criterion)

### Region of sale (WSTS)

**In plain words:** The region a chip sale is counted in: where the chips are shipped to the buyer, not where the seller is based or where the chip was made.

**Technically:** WSTS 'Geographic Billing': dollar value of net billings into each region by ship-to location between non-captive semiconductor manufacturers and their end customers, authorised distributors and end-product divisions (Americas, Europe, Japan, China, Asia Pacific/All Other).

**Related:** WSTS, Net billings

**Sources:** [SRC-0225](https://semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Green-Book-v18_Sample.pdf)

### Royalty

**In plain words:** A small payment made for each chip or device sold that uses someone else's technology.

**Technically:** Per-unit fee paid by a licensee on shipments of products incorporating licensed IP; may be a percentage of selling price or fixed per unit, sometimes with caps/minimums.

**Example:** Arm charges a royalty for every chip containing its products; Qualcomm charges a percentage of a device's wholesale price.

**Sources:** [SRC-0084](https://investors.arm.com/static-files/219a3b28-f209-4d74-8bc6-f9e026d55a95) [SRC-0080](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000080432825000085/qcom-20250928.htm)

### RTL

**In plain words:** The code-like description of what a chip's digital logic should do, written by designers.

**Technically:** Register-transfer level description of digital hardware in an HDL such as Verilog or VHDL.

**Related:** Logic synthesis

**Sources:** [SRC-0179](https://news.synopsys.com/2018-03-19-Synopsys-Introduces-Breakthrough-Fusion-Technology-to-Transform-the-RTL-to-GDSII-Flow)

### RTL-to-GDSII flow

**In plain words:** The whole chip-design pipeline from the logic description to the final layout file sent for manufacturing.

**Technically:** Digital implementation flow: synthesis, place-and-route, signoff, producing a GDSII/OASIS layout.

**Related:** GDSII, Tape-out

**Sources:** [SRC-0179](https://news.synopsys.com/2018-03-19-Synopsys-Introduces-Breakthrough-Fusion-Technology-to-Transform-the-RTL-to-GDSII-Flow)

### SECS/GEM

**In plain words:** The standard 'language' factory machines use to talk to the factory's control computers.

**Technically:** SEMI E5 (SECS-II messages), E37 (HSMS transport) and E30 (GEM behaviour model) for equipment-host communication.

**Related:** MES

**Sources:** [SRC-0182](https://store-us.semi.org/products/e03000-semi-e30-specification-for-the-generic-model-for-communications-and-control-of-manufacturing-equipment-gem)

### Section 232

**In plain words:** A US law allowing import restrictions or tariffs when imports are judged to threaten national security.

**Technically:** Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962; semiconductor investigation initiated April 1, 2025.

**Sources:** [SRC-0076](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/16/2025-06591/notice-of-request-for-public-comments-on-section-232-national-security-investigation-of-imports-of)

### Section 301

**In plain words:** A US law that lets the President retaliate against another country's unfair trade practices, e.g. with tariffs.

**Technically:** Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974; used in April 1987 to impose 100% tariffs on $300m of Japanese goods over the 1986 Semiconductor Arrangement.

**Sources:** [SRC-0046](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/archives/speech/memorandum-tariff-increases-japanese-semiconductor-products)

### SEMATECH

**In plain words:** A US joint research group of chipmakers, part-funded by the government, set up in 1987 to fight back against Japan.

**Technically:** Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology consortium formed August 1987 with anticipated federal support of $100m/year.

**Sources:** [SRC-0048](https://www.gao.gov/assets/rced-92-283.pdf)

### SEMI Standards

**In plain words:** Voluntary technical rules agreed by the chip equipment and materials industry so machines, materials and factories work together.

**Technically:** Consensus standards developed under SEMI's International Standards Program across equipment automation, materials, facilities, gases, lithography, packaging and traceability.

**Sources:** [SRC-0151](https://www.semi.org/en/products-services/standards/using-semi-standards)

### Semiconductor

**In plain words:** A material (usually silicon) whose ability to conduct electricity can be switched or tuned, and by extension the chips and devices made from it.

**Technically:** A device whose operation depends on variations in resistivity under an applied electric field (HS Ch.85 Note 12); US regulation: an integrated electronic device or system most commonly made of silicon, SiC or III-V compounds (15 CFR 231.115).

**Related:** Integrated circuit, Discrete semiconductor

**Sources:** [SRC-0017](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2022/c85.html) [SRC-0019](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-II/subchapter-C/part-231/subpart-A)

### Semiconductor Co-Investment Program

**In plain words:** Deals where outside investors pay for part of a chip factory in exchange for a share of it.

**Technically:** Intel's SCIP arrangements in which partners fund part of fab capex for minority stakes; reported as partner contributions offsetting gross capex.

**Sources:** [SRC-0099](https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0000050863-26-000009/q425earningsrelease.htm)

### Semiconductor IP

**In plain words:** Pre-designed building blocks of a chip that designers license instead of designing themselves.

**Technically:** Licensed design blocks/cell libraries paid via licence fees or royalties; ESD Alliance 'SIP' category.

**Sources:** [SRC-0028](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/news-events/tech-blog/from-foundry-to-fabless-an-overview-of-the-semiconductor-ecosystem/) [SRC-0022](https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/esd-alliance-reports-electronic-system-design-industry-posts-5.5-billion-dollars-in-revenue-in-q4-2025)

### Semiconductor manufacturing equipment

**In plain words:** The machines used to make and package chips.

**Technically:** Front-end (wafer processing, other front-end) and back-end (test, assembly & packaging) equipment tracked by SEMI/SEAJ.

**Sources:** [SRC-0020](https://www.semi.org/en/SEMI-Reports-Global-Semiconductor-Equipment-Billings-Reached-135-Billion-in-2025)

### Semiconductor materials

**In plain words:** Consumable inputs used to make chips, like silicon wafers, chemicals, gases and package substrates.

**Technically:** SEMI: wafer fabrication materials (e.g. photomask, photoresist, wet chemicals) and packaging materials (e.g. substrates, bonding wire).

**Sources:** [SRC-0021](https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/global-semiconductor-materials-market-revenue-reaches-record-73.2-billion-dollars-in-2025-semi-reports)

### Sensors and actuators (WSTS)

**In plain words:** Chips that measure things like temperature, pressure or motion, or that make small movements.

**Technically:** WSTS category of semiconductor devices whose electrical properties correlate with temperature, pressure, acceleration/yaw rate or magnetic field, plus actuators.

**Sources:** [SRC-0004](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Product_Classification_2021.pdf)

### Signoff

**In plain words:** The final set of checks confirming a chip design meets timing, power and manufacturing rules before it is sent to be made.

**Technically:** Final verification (static timing, parasitic extraction, DRC/LVS, power integrity) gating tape-out.

**Related:** Tape-out, Design rules

**Sources:** [SRC-0179](https://news.synopsys.com/2018-03-19-Synopsys-Introduces-Breakthrough-Fusion-Technology-to-Transform-the-RTL-to-GDSII-Flow)

### SoIC

**In plain words:** TSMC's technology for stacking chips directly on top of each other.

**Technically:** System on Integrated Chips: TSMC wafer-level 3D stacking platform with bond pitch below 10 µm.

**Related:** Hybrid bonding

**Sources:** [SRC-0169](https://3dfabric.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/SoIC.htm)

### STDF

**In plain words:** A common file format for storing chip test results.

**Technically:** Standard Test Data Format (Teradyne-originated, V4): binary record-based format for lot, wafer, part and test results.

**Related:** Automatic test equipment

**Sources:** [SRC-0188](https://www.kanwoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/std-spec.pdf)

### Supplement No. 4 to Part 742

**In plain words:** A list of 33 allied countries whose chip-tool exports from their own territory are exempt from certain new US licence requirements, because they have their own controls.

**Technically:** 'Countries Excluded From Certain Semiconductor Manufacturing Equipment License Requirements' (89 FR 96817), used in 742.4(a)(4)(ii)(B) to exclude SME exported from those countries by non-D:5-headquartered entities from SME FDP/de minimis licence requirements.

**Sources:** [SRC-0286](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-01/title-15.xml?part=742&appendix=Supplement%20No.%204%20to%20Part%20742) [SRC-0306](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-01/title-15.xml?part=742&section=742.4)

### Supply-demand balance (CoWoS)

**In plain words:** The point at which there is finally enough advanced-packaging capacity to meet all the orders; TSMC has repeatedly pushed back when it expects this.

**Technically:** Management language for when CoWoS/leading-edge capacity matches customer demand; TSMC's stated expectation moved from end-2024 to 2025-2026 to beyond 2026.

**Related:** CoWoS

### System-level test

**In plain words:** Testing a chip by running it as it would be used in a real device.

**Technically:** Functional ('mission mode') test of a packaged device in a system-like environment, complementing structural ATE test.

**Related:** Final test

**Sources:** [SRC-0186](https://investors.teradyne.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000950170-25-023784/ter-20241231.htm) [SRC-0187](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/0_2015-ITRS-2.0-Test-.pdf)

### T-glass

**In plain words:** A special glass fabric woven into chip package substrates to stop them warping when they heat up; very few factories can make it.

**Technically:** Low-coefficient-of-thermal-expansion glass fibre cloth used as reinforcement in BT and ABF-based IC substrates and core layers; dominated by Nitto Boseki (Nittobo).

**Related:** Package substrate, ABF

### Take-or-pay

**In plain words:** A contract where the buyer pays for an agreed amount even if it does not take it all.

**Technically:** Commitment structure with shortfall payments if purchased volume falls below the committed level. The term was not found in the filings read; GlobalFoundries describes 'significant customer commitments' in LTAs.

**Sources:** [SRC-0094](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1709048/000170904826000022/gfs-20251231.htm)

### Tape-out

**In plain words:** The moment a chip design is finished and handed over for mask-making and manufacturing.

**Technically:** Release of final signed-off layout data (GDSII/OASIS) to the foundry/mask shop; term originates from magnetic tape delivery.

**Related:** Mask set, Signoff

**Sources:** [SRC-0170](https://www.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/services/eFoundry) [SRC-0183](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/810136/000114036125045801/ef20057458_10k.htm)

### Temporary receipts

**In plain words:** Money a customer pays a chipmaker in advance to secure factory capacity, later refunded or offset.

**Technically:** TSMC's term for customer advance payments to retain specified capacity, settled by refund or offset against receivables per agreement.

**Sources:** [SRC-0077](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/sec-filings/2025_20F%20Report.pdf)

### Three-month moving average

**In plain words:** A smoothing method: each month's figure is the average of that month and the two before it.

**Technically:** Used by SIA when publishing WSTS monthly global sales.

**Sources:** [SRC-0008](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-increase-6-4-month-to-month-in-july/)

### Through-silicon via

**In plain words:** A tiny vertical wire drilled through a chip so signals can pass straight up and down a stack of chips.

**Technically:** Vertical electrical connection passing through a silicon die or wafer.

**Related:** HBM, 3D integration

**Sources:** [SRC-0176](https://news.skhynix.com/become-a-semiconductor-expert-with-sk-hynix-hbm/) [SRC-0196](https://www.imec-int.com/en/expertise/cmos-advanced/connect/3d-integration)

### Time-based licence

**In plain words:** Software rented for a fixed period (often several years) rather than bought outright.

**Technically:** EDA licence granting use for a defined term, often bundled with support as a subscription; revenue recognised over time or upfront depending on the arrangement.

**Sources:** [SRC-0081](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm) [SRC-0082](https://news.synopsys.com/2025-12-10-Synopsys-Posts-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025)

### Total processing performance (TPP)

**In plain words:** A single number the US government uses to measure how much computing a chip can do; above a set level, exporting the chip needs permission.

**Technically:** EAR metric of a chip's aggregate processing capability (replacing 'bits x TOPS' in October 2023); used in ECCN 3A090 (e.g. TPP >= 4800, or >= 1600 with performance density >= 5.92).

**Related:** Performance density, ECCN

**Sources:** [SRC-0282](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2023/10/25/2023-23055.txt) [SRC-0287](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/search/v1/results?query=%22performance%20density%22%205.92&per_page=5)

### TPU

**In plain words:** Google's own AI chip, used in Google's data centres and rented to cloud customers such as Anthropic.

**Technically:** Google-designed AI accelerator ASIC family (seventh generation 'Ironwood' GA in late 2025); physical design and supply handled with Broadcom; offered via Google Cloud.

**Related:** XPU, ASIC

### Trainium

**In plain words:** Amazon's own AI chip, used inside Amazon Web Services and by customers such as Anthropic.

**Technically:** AWS-designed AI training/inference accelerator family (Trainium2 in volume 2025; Trainium3 from 2026) deployed in AWS data centres, e.g. Project Rainier.

**Related:** XPU, TPU

### Transistor

**In plain words:** A tiny electronic switch or amplifier; modern chips contain billions of them.

**Technically:** A three-terminal semiconductor device that amplifies or switches signals; first demonstrated as a germanium point-contact device at Bell Labs in December 1947.

**Sources:** [SRC-0032](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/invention-of-the-point-contact-transistor/)

### Trusted supplier

**In plain words:** A chip designer, factory, or packaging or test company certified by the US Defense Department to handle sensitive defense chips securely.

**Technically:** DMEA-accredited supplier (design, aggregation, broker, mask, foundry, post-processing, packaging/assembly, test) assuring IC integrity and confidentiality.

**Sources:** [SRC-0194](https://www.acq.osd.mil/asds/dmea/tapo/trusted-supplier-programs.html)

### Turnkey design service

**In plain words:** A company that takes a customer's chip idea and handles design, manufacturing, packaging and testing through to finished chips.

**Technically:** ASIC service model with entry points (spec-in, RTL-in, GDSII-in, KGD-in) through to delivered product, managing foundry and ATP on the customer's behalf.

**Sources:** [SRC-0219](https://www.guc-asic.com/en/about/us)

### UCIe

**In plain words:** A shared technical standard for how chiplets from different makers talk to each other inside a package.

**Technically:** Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express: open die-to-die interconnect specification (v3.0 current) covering physical layer, protocol and management.

**Related:** Chiplet

**Sources:** [SRC-0175](https://www.uciexpress.org/)

### US person (EAR)

**In plain words:** Anyone the US export rules treat as American: US citizens, green-card holders, people in the US and US companies. Some chip rules limit what they may do for Chinese chip factories.

**Technically:** EAR term covering US citizens, permanent residents, protected individuals, US-organised entities and persons in the US; 744.6(c)(2) requires licences for their support of specified advanced-IC production in China.

**Sources:** [SRC-0279](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2022/10/13/2022-21658.txt) [SRC-0296](https://www.ajot.com/news/ban-on-us-talent-at-china-chip-firms-thwarts-xias-key-ambition)

### Validated End-User (VEU)

**In plain words:** A pre-approval that lets a vetted foreign factory receive controlled US goods without its suppliers asking for a licence each time.

**Technically:** EAR authorisation (15 CFR 748.15) under which approved end users receive eligible items without the supplier obtaining an individual licence; revoked for Samsung, SK hynix and Intel Dalian China fabs effective 31 Dec 2025.

**Sources:** [SRC-0293](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2025/09/02/2025-16735.txt) [SRC-0294](https://media.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-closes-export-controls-loophole-foreign-owned-semiconductor-fabs-china)

### Wafer

**In plain words:** A thin round slice of silicon on which many chips are made at once.

**Technically:** A thin disk of single-crystal semiconductor (commonly 200mm or 300mm diameter) serving as the substrate for IC fabrication; capacity is often measured in wafers per month.

**Sources:** [SRC-0044](https://50th.itri.org.tw/en/history/semiconductors/2/)

### Wafer acceptance test

**In plain words:** Electrical tests on special test structures on the wafer that show whether the manufacturing process ran correctly; foundries share this data with customers.

**Technically:** WAT/PCM/e-test: parametric measurement of scribe-line test structures after fab completion.

**Related:** Wafer sort

**Sources:** [SRC-0170](https://www.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/services/eFoundry) [SRC-0189](https://www.pdf.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-data-101-an-introduction/)

### Wafer map

**In plain words:** A map showing which chips on a wafer passed or failed tests, used to track each chip through packaging.

**Technically:** Substrate map of die XY coordinates with bin/test results, standardized in SEMI E142.

**Related:** Wafer sort

**Sources:** [SRC-0181](https://www.semi.org/en/standards-watch-2026-apr/major-revision-underway-for-semi-e142) [SRC-0190](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/opcenter/execution/semiconductor/)

### Wafer sort

**In plain words:** Testing each chip while it is still on the wafer, using fine needles (probes) to touch its contacts.

**Technically:** Electrical wafer probe test using ATE and probe cards; results recorded as wafer/bin maps.

**Related:** Final test, Wafer map

**Sources:** [SRC-0186](https://investors.teradyne.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000950170-25-023784/ter-20241231.htm) [SRC-0189](https://www.pdf.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-data-101-an-introduction/) [SRC-0187](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/0_2015-ITRS-2.0-Test-.pdf)

### Wassenaar Arrangement

**In plain words:** A club of 42 countries that agree on lists of sensitive technologies to control when exporting.

**Technically:** Multilateral export control regime (since 1996) maintaining the Munitions List and the List of Dual-Use Goods and Technologies, implemented nationally by consensus.

**Sources:** [SRC-0136](https://www.wassenaar.org/about-us/)

### WSTS

**In plain words:** The industry body that collects chip companies' sales data and publishes the standard 'semiconductor market' figure.

**Technically:** World Semiconductor Trade Statistics, non-profit founded 1986; members that design and sell chips under their own name report revenue monthly.

**Sources:** [SRC-0001](https://www.wsts.org/esraCMS/extension/media/f/WST/7618/WSTS_FC-Release-2026-May.pdf) [SRC-0005](https://www.wsts.org/61/OVERVIEW)

### XPU

**In plain words:** Broadcom's word for an AI chip it designs together with one big customer, just for that customer.

**Technically:** A customer-specific AI accelerator ASIC co-developed by a design partner (e.g. Broadcom, Marvell) for a hyperscaler or AI lab; the partner handles physical design, IP, foundry and packaging and books the chip revenue.

**Related:** ASIC, Hyperscaler

### Yield

**In plain words:** Out of all the chips made on a wafer, the share that actually work.

**Technically:** Percentage of functional die per processed wafer (die yield); also used for line yield (wafers surviving the process) and final test yield.

**Related:** Die, Known good die

**Sources:** [SRC-0163](https://newsroom.intel.com/tech101/explaining-common-chip-terms)

### Yield management system

**In plain words:** Software that pulls together manufacturing and test data to find why chips fail and improve yield.

**Technically:** YMS: analytics platform integrating fab (inline, metrology, defect, WAT) and test (sort, final) data for yield learning.

**Related:** Yield, Wafer acceptance test

**Sources:** [SRC-0189](https://www.pdf.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-data-101-an-introduction/)
