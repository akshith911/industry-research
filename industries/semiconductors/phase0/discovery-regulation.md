## 2. Authorities and instruments by jurisdiction

### United States
- **Bureau of Industry and Security (Commerce):** Oct 2022 advanced computing/SME rule, 87 FR 62186 (c01–c03). <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2022/10/13/2022-21658/implementation-of-additional-export-controls-certain-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-manufacturing-items>. Dec 2024 SME/HBM/FDP rule, 89 FR 96790 (c04). <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/12/05/2024-28270/foreign-produced-direct-product-rule-additions-and-refinements-to-controls-for-advanced-computing>. The 50% affiliates rule was issued Sept 2025 and is **suspended from 10 Nov 2025 to 9 Nov 2026** (c05–c06). <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/12/2025-19846/one-year-suspension-of-expansion-of-end-user-controls-for-affiliates-of-certain-listed-entities>. The AI Diffusion Rule has **not been enforced since May 2025**. A formal rescission was not confirmed (c09–c10). <https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-announces-rescission-biden-era-artificial-intelligence-diffusion-rule-strengthens>. From 15 Jan 2026, exports of H200-class chips to China and Macau get **case-by-case review** (c07–c08). <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/15/2026-00789/revision-to-license-review-policy-for-advanced-computing-commodities>
- **CHIPS Program Office (NIST/Commerce), now under the US Investment Accelerator:** $52.7bn in total, of which $39bn is for incentives and $11bn for R&D (c11–c12). <https://www.nist.gov/document/chips-america-fact-sheet-federal-incentives>. Guardrails, 15 CFR 231 (c13). <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/09/25/2023-20471/preventing-the-improper-use-of-chips-act-funding>. Investment Accelerator executive order, 31 Mar 2025 (c16). <https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/establishing-the-united-states-investment-accelerator/>. Intel 9.9% equity deal, Aug 2025 (c17). <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086325000129/a08222025form8-kex991.htm>
- **Congress / IRS (Treasury):** The 26 USC 48D credit is **35%** after P.L. 119-21, with 25% for facilities placed in service before 2026. It applies only where construction begins by 31 Dec 2026 (c14–c15). <https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A26+section%3A48D+edition%3Aprelim%29>, <https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/correction-to-the-2025-instructions-for-form-3468-of-the-applicability-of-the-35-rate-on-line-1c-in-part-iv-advanced-manufacturing-investment-credit-under-section-48d>
- **President (Section 232):** A 25% duty on certain advanced computing chips has applied since 15 Jan 2026, with broad exemptions (c19–c21). <https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/01/adjusting-imports-of-semiconductors-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment-and-their-derivative-products-into-the-united-states/>. A phase two was **signalled in Sept 2026, but no proclamation had been found** (c22, tier-3 only).
- **USTR (Section 301):** The action on Chinese semiconductors is 0% from 23 Dec 2025 and rises on 23 Jun 2027 to a rate not yet announced. This is on top of the existing 50% (c23–c24). <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/29/2025-23912/notice-of-action-chinas-acts-policies-and-practices-related-to-targeting-of-the-semiconductor>
- **Treasury (Outbound Investment Security Program):** In effect since 2 Jan 2025. It covers semiconductors in China, Hong Kong and Macau (c25). <https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/international/outbound-investment-program>
- **SEC:** Conflict minerals disclosure on Form SD (c26). <https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/conflict-minerals-disclosure>

### European Union
- **European Parliament and Council / Commission (DG CNECT):** The Chips Act, Regulation (EU) 2023/1781, has been in force since 21 Sept 2023 and has 3 pillars (c27–c28). The Commission has approved 18 State aid decisions covering more than €32bn of investment (c29). Chips Act 2.0 was **proposed on 3 Jun 2026 and is not yet law** (c30–c31). Sources: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1781/oj>, <https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-chips-act>, <https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/proposal-chips-act-20>
- **Commission (DG Trade):** The dual-use Regulation 2021/821 got a 2026 Annex I update that adds semiconductor manufacturing equipment and advanced ICs. It was **in scrutiny as of Sept 2026** (c32, c43). <https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/news/2026-update-eu-control-list-dual-use-items-2026-09-14_en>. Revised FDI screening was politically agreed on 11 Dec 2025 and covers semiconductors (c33). <https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/11/foreign-direct-investment-council-and-parliament-reached-political-agreement-to-improve-fdi-screening/>
- **ECHA / DG CLIMA:** The PFAS restriction and the F-gas Regulation 2024/573 apply. How they treat semiconductor uses was **not established** (c34–c35).

### Netherlands
- **Minister for Foreign Trade and Development (Ministry of Foreign Affairs):** National licensing of advanced SME, starting with DUV lithography, since 1 Sept 2023. It was extended on 7 Sept 2024, and metrology/inspection equipment was added on 1 Apr 2025 (c36–c38). Sources: <https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2024/09/06/the-netherlands-expands-export-control-measure-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment>, <https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2025/01/15/klever-export-controls-on-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment-to-be-tightened>

### Japan
- **METI:** Since July 2023, 23 SME types are controlled under the Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Act (c39). <https://www.meti.go.jp/english/report/data/wp2024/pdf/2-1-2.pdf>. Public support for AI and semiconductors is planned at ¥10tn or more through FY2030 (c40). <https://www.meti.go.jp/english/speeches/press_conferences/2024/1112001.html>. In Nov 2025 Rapidus was selected under the information-processing promotion law, with a ¥100bn IPA investment (c41, reported claim).

### China
- **MOFCOM (with the General Administration of Customs):** Gallium and germanium have needed export licences since 1 Aug 2023 (c44–c45). <https://www.usitc.gov/publications/332/executive_briefings/ebot_germanium_and_gallium.pdf>. Notice 2024 No. 46 banned some exports to the US; that ban is **suspended until 27 Nov 2026** (c46–c47). Notice 2025 No. 61 set the rare-earth 0.1% rule; it and related announcements are **suspended until 10 Nov 2026** (c48–c49). MOFCOM opened an anti-dumping investigation into US analog ICs in Sept 2025, with the **outcome unknown** (c51–c52).
- **Ministry of Finance and state investors:** Big Fund III was registered with CNY 344bn in May 2024 (c50).
- **Cybersecurity Review Office / CAC:** The Micron review in May 2023 (c53).

### South Korea
- **National Assembly / MOEF:** The K-Chips Act amendment of Feb 2025 set a 20% facility-investment tax credit for large firms and 30% for SMEs (c54). The Semiconductor Special Act passed on 29 Jan 2026 and was **expected to take effect in Q3 2026. Entry into force was not confirmed** (c55). These claims rest on tier-3 news only.

### Taiwan
- **Legislative Yuan / MOEA:** Article 10-2 of the Statute for Industrial Innovation gives a 25% R&D credit and a 5% credit for advanced-process equipment. Applications opened in Feb 2024 (c56, tier-2). **MOEA International Trade Administration:** In June 2025, 601 entities were added to the Strategic High-Tech Commodities entity list, including Huawei and SMIC (c57).

### India
- **MeitY / India Semiconductor Mission:** The Semicon India programme, approved Dec 2021, has a ₹76,000 crore budget and offers up to 50% fiscal support (c58). As of Dec 2025, 10 projects worth ₹1.60 lakh crore had been approved (c59). ISM 2.0 has ₹1,000 crore for FY2026-27 (c60). <https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2224839&reg=3&lang=1>

### Multilateral
- **Wassenaar Arrangement:** 42 states maintain a shared dual-use list, and each state implements it in national law (c42). <https://www.wassenaar.org/about-us/>

### Standards bodies
- **SEMI International Standards Program:** Voluntary consensus standards for equipment automation, materials, gases, facilities, lithography, packaging and traceability (c61–c62). <https://www.semi.org/en/products-services/standards/using-semi-standards>
- **JEDEC:** Standards for memory (DDR, LPDDR, HBM, flash), packaging and ESD, with more than 380 members (c63). <https://www.jedec.org/about-jedec>
- Found but not evidenced in this run: **IEC TC 47** (semiconductor devices) and the **Automotive Electronics Council** (AEC-Q100). Both pages failed to load (403 or TLS error). ISO and IEEE were not researched.
