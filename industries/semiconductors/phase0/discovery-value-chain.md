These roles overlap. One company can hold several of them: Samsung is an IDM and a foundry, ASE is an OSAT and an EMS provider, and Arm licenses IP and now also sells chips. The ontology should let a company carry several roles.

## 2. Value-chain stages in order

1. **Pre-competitive research.** Universities, consortia and company R&D. The industry spent about USD 90bn on R&D in 2019.
2. **Raw materials.** Polysilicon, gases (neon from air separation) and chemicals. The purification step is done by specialists.
3. **Wafers and other fab materials.** Silicon wafers (12,973 MSI shipped in 2025, USD 11.4bn), photoresists, wet chemicals, gases and photomasks. Fab materials were USD 45.8bn in 2025.
4. **Equipment.** More than 50 tool types. Billings were USD 135.1bn in 2025, of which China took USD 49.3bn. Service and spares continue after the sale.
5. **EDA and core IP.** Design software (about USD 6.8bn in 2019, 96% from US firms) and licensable blocks (about USD 3.9bn in 2019).
6. **Chip design.** Specification, logic design, physical design, and verification. Design was about 65% of industry R&D and 53% of value added in 2019. It is done by fabless firms, IDMs, hyperscalers and ASIC service houses.
7. **Mask making.** Each design is turned into a set of custom photomasks by captive or merchant mask shops.
8. **Wafer fabrication (front end).** Done by foundries and IDMs. This stage was about 65% of industry capex in 2019, and one fab costs USD 5–20bn.
9. **Assembly, packaging and test (back end).** Dicing, packaging and advanced packaging (including substrates), then wafer probe, final test and system-level test. Done by OSATs, IDMs and foundries. At least 81% of capacity is in Asia.
10. **Distribution.** Chips reach buyers either directly or through authorized distributors. The open market of brokers is a secondary channel.
11. **System integration.** EMS providers and OEMs build chips into boards and products.
12. **End markets.** In 2024 demand split as computers/AI 34.9% and automotive 9.9%, with the rest in communications, consumer, industrial and government (per WSTS end-use survey via SIA).
13. **Post-sale.** Equipment service and upgrades (ASML installed-base sales were EUR 8.2bn in 2025), plus chip royalties that keep flowing after sale (Arm royalties).
