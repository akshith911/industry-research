# RU-0011 notes (res-RU-0011-ee70)

- Fabless-share series: IC Insights public bulletins (2013, 2021, 2022) and TechInsights' June 2025 public excerpt (IC Insights' successor) give 1999 ~7%, 2002 13%, 2003 14%, 2009 >20%, 2010 24%, 2012 27.8%, 2014 >30%, 2019 29.9%/30.5%, 2020 32.8%, 2021 34.8%, 2024 50%. EDN 2003 adds 1997 6% (chip revenue). GSA 1H 2008 20% of semiconductor sales. All are research-firm/association estimates; methodology not public.
- Pure-play foundry market: 2000 US$10.8bn (EDN citing IC Insights), 2016 US$50.0bn (IC Insights), total foundry incl. IDM foundry 2024 US$151.4bn (TechInsights). Note the 2024 figure is a different population (includes IDM foundry services).
- OTA 1983 'International Competitiveness in Electronics' semiconductor chapters (parts 7, 8, 17) were blocked by Cloudflare for both web_extract and the browser; only chapter 2 (part 5) was readable.
- Existing related claims reused rather than repeated: CLM-0104/0105 (UMC), CLM-0106/0107 (TSMC founding), CLM-0108 (Conway), CLM-0109 (FSA->GSA), CLM-0110 (Arm 1990), CLM-0112/CLM-2312 (AMD-ATIC), CLM-0113 (IDM 2.0), CLM-0152 (unknown fabless series - now largely answered by this batch).
