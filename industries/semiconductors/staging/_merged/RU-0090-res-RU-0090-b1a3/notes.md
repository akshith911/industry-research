# RU-0090 business-model catalogue (researcher notes, res-RU-0090-b1a3)

Every row is based on one company's FY2025/FY2026 filing. None of these figures is a category average. Keys refer to claims in this batch.

| Participant type | Sample firm | What it sells | Pricing unit / basis | Contract form | Revenue timing | Recurring? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pure-play foundry | GlobalFoundries, TSMC, UMC | Finished wafers + NRE, masks, bump/test, IP | **Per wafer for the design** (GF, gf_perwafer); TSMC/UMC do not state the unit (tsmc_unit_unknown) | PO + MSA (volume pricing formulas, yield adjustments); LTAs with breakage fees; capacity prepayments (tsmc_tr) | Point in time at shipment/delivery; NRE over time | Transactional; LTAs give volume visibility |
| IDM foundry business | Intel Foundry | Wafers and back-end services, mostly to Intel's own product groups | Internal transfer price set to approximate market prices | Intersegment; US$307m external revenue | At wafer sort; back-end at completion of assembly/test milestones | n/a |
| IDM | Intel, TI, Microchip | Chips | Unit price less concessions/rebates | POs; distributor agreements (price concessions, stock rotation); LTSAs with deposits | Point in time; consignment sales recognised when the customer pulls stock | Transactional (+ small technology-licence royalties at Microchip) |
| Fabless | NVIDIA, Qualcomm QCT | Chips and systems | Unit price less rebates and MDFs | POs | Point in time | Transactional |
| Device-level patent licensor | Qualcomm QTL | Patent portfolio access | % of the device's wholesale price, with per-unit floors/caps | Multi-year licence | As licensees sell; paid the following quarter | Recurring (royalty) |
| IP licensor | Arm, Ceva, Rambus | Cores/architecture/interface IP | Licence fee (per design or subscription) + per-chip royalty | Licences of 1-10 years (Rambus); architecture licences of 3-20 years (Arm) | Licence: point in time or ratable; royalties accrued when chips ship | Royalties 53% of revenue at Arm, 42% at Ceva |
| EDA vendor | Cadence, Synopsys | Software, hardware, IP | Time-based licences invoiced quarterly; FSA committed spend; remix rights. **No seat price disclosed** | Multi-year | 76% over time (Cadence) | 80% recurring (Cadence) |
| Mask maker | Photronics | Custom mask sets | Negotiated per order; no list prices; share-of-orders understandings | POs; backlog of 1-2 weeks | Over time (no alternative use) | Transactional, driven by the number of designs |
| OSAT | Amkor | Packaging and test services on consigned wafers | Customer-specific negotiated price per service; **unit not disclosed** | Billed at completion of each step; rising customer advances | Over time (2-3 weeks) | Per-order |
| WFE maker | AMAT, KLA, ASML | Tools, spares, service, upgrades, software | Per system; slot reservation and down payment; 70-90% paid at shipment, the rest at acceptance | VPAs (up to 5 years at ASML) with discounts and credits | Tools point in time; service over time | Service is about 23% of revenue (AMAT, KLA) |
| Test-equipment maker | Teradyne | Testers + service | Per system | POs | 13% over time | Low |
| Manufacturing software | PDF Solutions, KLA software | Licences, SaaS, Gainshare | Perpetual or term licence; subscription; per wafer volume (Gainshare) | Multi-year | Mixed | 94% "recurring" (company definition) |
| Materials | Entegris, AXT, Siltronic | Consumables, substrates, gallium, wafers | Negotiated formula, list or fixed price (AXT); LTA vs spot (Siltronic) | Short POs (AXT); LTAs with prepayments (Siltronic) | Point in time | Transactional; LTAs at wafer makers |
| Specialty gas | Linde | Gases | Index-linked pricing; 10-20-year on-site total-requirement contracts; 3-7-year merchant contracts | Long-term supply contracts | On-site over time; merchant point in time | Contracted volumes (on-site) |
| Authorized distributor | Avnet, Arrow | Components | Resale spread; supplier price protection and rebates booked in cost of sales | Franchise agreements; agent (net) for services | Point in time | Transactional |
| ASIC house | Alchip | NRE + turnkey chips | Milestone NRE + production units | Per-programme | n/a (press releases only) | NRE was 30-40% of sales in 3Q25 and under 20% in 1Q23 |
